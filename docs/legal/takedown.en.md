# Reports and takedown

How to tell us about content that is wrong or unlawful, and what happens next (EU Digital Services Act, notice and action). Draft for review before the public launch.

## Reporting an event

- **In the app:** on any event, **Report**. Choose what's wrong: cancelled, venue closed, moved, wrong details, inappropriate, unlawful, or other, with an optional note.
- **By email:** {{contactEmail}}, with the event's link, what's wrong, and for unlawful content why you think so. Add your name and email unless the report is about child sexual abuse material, where you may stay anonymous.
- **Organiser posts** have their own Report button.

## What happens

- Reports that an event is off (cancelled, moved, venue closed) show to everyone at once as a warning with how many people reported it, until a moderator confirms or dismisses them.
- **Unlawful or inappropriate content** goes to a moderator, who decides within 48 hours: hide or remove it, or keep it with a reason.
- The person who reported (if we know how to reach them) and the person who added the content are told the decision and the reason.
- Accounts that repeatedly add unlawful content or spam lose trusted status (their events wait for review again), and their private events can be stopped.

## Disagreeing

Write to {{contactEmail}} within six months of a decision; a different moderator looks at it again. You can also use any out-of-court dispute body certified under the DSA, or a court.

## Websites we read

If your site is read by our crawler and you want it to stop or to change what we show, see [the crawler page]({{siteUrl}}/crawler). We stop within 48 hours of a request.

## Personal data

To have your name removed from an event listing, or for any other data request, see [the privacy policy](/legal/privacy.en) (§7–8).
