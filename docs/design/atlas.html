Lit map and the local atlas: a grey veil with soft holes for your reach and the places you have been, fading as you zoom out, and a wordless settings row to export, import and clear the atlas.

reach500 m

Drag to pan, tap to add a visit. Opens are faint, arrivals strong, repeats brighter, nothing fades with time. The reach is always lit. The veil fades from a 1 km reach and is gone at 5 km (city scale). Street names sit above the veil.

Settings sheet: atlas row (between language and About)

Left: export (saves happow-atlas-2026-10-23.json, or the share sheet). Right: import a file. The number is how many cells are lit. Far right: hold the bin for 1.5 s to clear.
