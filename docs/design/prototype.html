Happow map prototype

Design reference for M6 (slices E to H). Drag the right rail to change reach and the bottom line to move in time. The calendar icons switch Today, 7 days and 14 days. Tap pins, the dice and the ● counter. Demo time: Friday 23 October, 16:10.

Big filled = on now · ring = time left, full at 2 h+ · dotted ring = no fixed end · outlined = starts within 1 h · small dot = later · dashed = late for a fixed start, or closed before you'd arrive · grey map = not yet yours, colour = your reach and places you've been

Normative (match these)

  • Range icons: calendar frame with one filled day (Today), one filled row (7 days), two filled rows (14 days). Exact geometry in docs/design/icons/; 1.6 stroke, round caps.
  • Range icons sit at the left end of the time line. Today steps by hour; 7 and 14 days step by day, with a per-day histogram. Tapping the active icon again shows the whole range.
  • Pins: 34 px live with a remaining-time ring (full at 2 h or more, 8 % minimum), 26 px within the hour, 10 px dots later. Outlined in the live colour when starting within the hour; dashed when you'd be late or miss it.
  • One live colour, used only for "on now". Violet is the default; Red and Mono are presets. Night land is pure black.
  • Chrome positions: live counter top-left, dice and filters top-right, reach rail on the right edge, locate and list below it, list as an overlay over the map.

Illustrative (do not copy)

  • The street grid, river, parks and sample events; the real app draws the MapLibre basemap and live data.
  • Line icons for categories and controls are Tabler Icons (MIT), used here as the reference style for the M6 glyph set.
